This release brings significant improvements to performance, parallelism, and use of available resources.

The numbers below were measured on a MacBook Pro M5 (18-core CPU), using the invoice document from the Getting Started tutorial, across various levels of parallelism, over a sample of 5,000 documents.

- Reduced memory usage and allocated object count by 85%.
- Improved single-threaded performance by around 15%.
- Substantially improved parallel scaling: throughput previously peaked at 4 threads and flattened beyond that, and now scales with available CPU cores — around 4x better scaling.
- Improved performance and scalability for text-heavy documents.
- Improved cache usage (mainly for fonts) and reduced locking.
- Improved reliability of the Companion App integration.
- Improved visual position of text underline and double underline.
- Updated the Skia native dependency to version m152.
